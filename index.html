ACE density and speed set dynamic pressure; they do not supply the interplanetary magnetic-field direction needed for a full magnetosphere model. GOES particle fluxes and Kp are instrument/index measurements, not global particle maps or satellite-specific dose. X-ray flux is shown as incident solar irradiance, not surface heating.
