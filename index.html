ACE density and speed set dynamic pressure; they do not supply the interplanetary magnetic-field direction needed for a full magnetosphere model. GOES particle fluxes and Kp are instrument/index measurements, not global particle maps or satellite-specific dose. X-ray flux measures incident solar irradiance; no surface heating is estimated. Spacecraft mass, onboard temperature, attitude, and hardware telemetry are shown only when a source actually publishes them; none are inferred here.
